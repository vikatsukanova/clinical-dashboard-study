Design system

Clinical interface

Tokens, components, and the chart layout for a fictional patient record. Designers use the roles below. Developers compose the same pieces in Storybook.

Record Jordan Hale, 65. Not a real patient, clinician, or product.
Structure Foundations, atoms, molecules, organisms, one template, two pages.
Type Inter. Color pairs meet WCAG AA for text.

01

How it is built

Each level only knows the level under it. The template owns the grid. The page decides which view fills the middle column.

Level Job Pieces
Foundations Color, type, space, radius, shadow CSS variables on :root
Atoms One control, no layout opinions Icon, icon button, count, tab, button, chip, status, text controls
Molecules Atoms with one job Field, choice, lab metric, timeline item
Organisms A section of the chart Patient record, diagnosis timeline, assessment form, clinical results
Template Three slots and the small-screen switch Chart
Pages One record, one view Dashboard, assessment

One primary button in a region. It is the next step, such as Start assessment or Publish. Secondary saves a draft. Ghost leaves the flow. A chip names a fact. A status names where an assessment is. Do not use one as the other.

06

Chart

The template measures its own width. Wide screens show three columns. Narrow screens show one section at a time.

Patient320pxIdentity, history, risks, medications
WorkCentered, up to 600pxTimeline on the dashboard. The form during an assessment.
Clinical384pxExam, labs, ECG, echo, cath
WidthWhat changes
1041px and upThree columns. Gap and page padding are 24px. The bottom bar is hidden.
1040px and underOne section. The timeline is the default. A fixed bar switches Patient, Timeline, and Clinical.
720px and underForm actions stack and take the full width.

Result cards carry an expand mark in the top right. It is decorative. The patient column does not. Section titles for exam, labs, ECG, echo, and cath sit above their cards.

Accessibility

RuleHow it is met
NameIcon buttons require a label. Status writes “In progress” or “Published”. Choices and fields have visible labels.
Focus2px brand outline, 2px offset, on buttons, tabs, fields, choices, and the section bar.
LandmarksPatient and clinical results are labeled regions. The section bar is a navigation landmark.
Not color aloneLab intent includes the signed delta. Status includes the word and a decorative icon.
CheckEvery story runs axe. A failure blocks the story. The region rule is the only one turned off, because Storybook’s canvas is not a page landmark.
